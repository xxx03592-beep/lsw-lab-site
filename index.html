FAQ

Questions
brands ask us.

How is this different from a design subscription?

A design subscription waits for your requests. We do the research, write the concepts and choose the angles, then design the ad visuals. You get ideas, not just production.

How do you use AI? Will it look like AI?

AI handles production; the direction, selection and finishing are done by hand. We start from your real product photos, build a direction board you approve, generate and select images against it, then retouch each one to correct details, fabric and fit. Anything that looks off doesn't leave the studio.

Why statics and carousels?

They're the fastest way to test a new angle on Meta. One concept can become several variations and be live before a video would be through pre-production.

What do you need from us?

Your product photos, your website and a few references. A short brief helps, but we can write one with you.

Do you write the ad copy?

No, by design. We deliver clean visuals with no text baked in, so you keep full control of your message and can test any headline on the same image.

Do you run media buying?

No. We focus entirely on creative. We work alongside your in-house team or agency, and use your results to shape the next batch.

Who owns the creative?

You can use all final files commercially without restriction, and we transfer any rights we hold in them to you.
